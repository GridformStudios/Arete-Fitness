ἀρετή  a·re·tē

/ ah-reh-TAY /  ·  noun, Ancient Greek

Meaning

Excellence of any kind — a person or thing fulfilling its purpose and performing to the height of its ability. To Aristotle it was not an inborn gift but a habit, built by repetition.
